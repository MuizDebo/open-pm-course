## Introduction

The delivery pack is where the strategy meets an engineering team. It takes the feature at
the top of your backlog and does everything a PM does to get it built and shipped: specify
it so a team can build it, sketch it so a designer can argue with it, plan it so the team
knows what the next two weeks hold, prepare its launch, and imagine how it fails before it
does.

It draws on three Course 5 projects: 5.1.4 The PRD, 5.2.6 Sprint plan and 5.4.4 Launch
pack, and on the wireframing you practised in lesson 5.1.3 Mockups and wireframes. If you
carried the same product through Course 5, this pack is about rewriting the PRD against
the new goals sheet and checking that the plan still fits a real team. If you are fresh or
on Restock, it is Course 5 in a week, and the PRD deserves two sessions on its own.

## Before you start

The joint that fails most often in capstones is between the strategy pack and this one. The
PRD specifies a feature that is not the top of the backlog, or serves a goal that is not on
the goals sheet, or quietly grows to include three features. Before you write a word, write
at the top of the PRD: this feature is backlog item one, it serves goal two, and it is
out of scope to do anything else. Then hold to it.

**For Restock**, the first feature has to work on a low-end Android phone over metered
data, mostly inside WhatsApp, for a shop owner who will not read instructions and a
wholesaler who will not install anything. Your wireframes may be message flows rather than
screens. Your launch plan has to say how the first twenty shop owners and the first three
wholesalers get onboarded by hand, because they will be.

**If reusing Course 5 work**, the sprint plan is the artifact most likely to be fiction.
Re-estimate with lesson 5.2.3 Estimation and why it fails in mind, and cut the second sprint
until it fits.

## Assignment

<div class="assignment">

1. **PRD.** For the single feature at the top of the strategy pack's backlog, in the form
   from lesson 5.1.1 Requirements: problem and evidence from the discovery pack, the goal it
   serves from the goals sheet, user stories with acceptance criteria, success metrics,
   non-goals, open questions, and risks. Two to four pages. If this feature involves a
   model, say so here and leave the behaviour spec for the AI pack in project 9.6.
2. **Wireframes.** Low fidelity, every screen or message in the main flow plus the two most
   likely error states. Paper and a phone camera are fine. For Restock, show the WhatsApp
   conversation as it would actually read, both sides.
3. **Sprint plan.** Epics and stories from the PRD, estimated, laid out across two sprints
   for a team you describe in a sentence. Mark what is in sprint one, what is in sprint two,
   and what you cut to make it fit. If you can, run a mock sprint planning with another
   learner from Discussions and note what they challenged.
4. **Launch plan and release notes.** The launch tier, the checklist, who needs to know
   what and when, the rollout and rollback plan, and the release notes as the user would
   read them. For Restock, the plan for onboarding the first users by hand is part of this.
5. **Pre-mortem.** It is three months after launch and the feature failed. Write the five
   most likely reasons in the order you believe them, and for the top two, what you are
   changing in the PRD or the plan now. Then go and change it.
6. **Decision record.** One page: the scope you cut from the PRD, who wanted it, and why it
   waits.
7. **Pack summary.** A `README.md` in `9-capstone/delivery`: two sentences per artifact and
   the link to each.

#### Extra credit

- Write the retrospective you expect to run after sprint one, using one of the formats from
  lesson 5.4.2 Retrospectives, with the action items you already know you will need.
- Have someone with engineering experience read the PRD cold and list every question they
  would ask before starting. Add the answers to the PRD and the questions to the pack as
  evidence.

</div>

## Submitting your work

Post a link to your artifact in this project's thread in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MuizDebo/open-pm-course/discussions). Review at
least two other submissions while you are there. For capstone packs, check the joint: is the
PRD's feature the top of their backlog, and does it serve a goal on their goals sheet? Say
so either way.

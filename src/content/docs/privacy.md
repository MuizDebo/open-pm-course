This course is free and open source. To make it better, the site counts a few things about how
it is used. It does not track you.

## What is counted

- **Pages viewed**, so we can see which lessons people read most and where they stop.
- **Search terms** typed into the site search, so we can see what people come looking for and
  write the lessons that are missing. Please do not type personal information into the search.
- **Outside links opened** from lessons, so we can keep the readings that people use and replace
  the ones they skip.
- **Cross-references followed** between lessons, and whether a lesson was read as far as its
  assignment.
- **Study plans downloaded** from the planner in lesson 0.2. Only the fact of a download is
  counted. The days, times and dates you choose never leave your browser.

## What is not

- No cookies, no advertising trackers, no fingerprinting, and no account or name attached to
  anything.
- Nothing is sold or shared.
- Your submissions in GitHub Discussions are governed by GitHub's own privacy policy, not this
  page.

## How

Counting uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), an open-source analytics service that
does not use cookies or store personal data. The code that sends the counts is in the
repository at `public/analytics.js` and anyone can read it.

Questions or concerns: open an issue in the
[repository](https://github.com/MuizDebo/open-pm-course/issues).

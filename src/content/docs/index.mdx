## What you get

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Ten courses, one path" icon="open-book">
    Foundations, the PM mind, discovery, strategy, delivery, data, AI product management, career, and a capstone. 109 lessons and 29 projects, about 30 weeks at 8 to 10 hours a week.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Projects, not quizzes" icon="pencil">
    Every course ends in artifacts a working PM actually produces: audience maps, PRDs, roadmaps, eval suites, launch plans. Submit them in GitHub Discussions and review others.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI product management throughout" icon="rocket">
    The largest course in the path. How models work, where AI belongs, designing for uncertainty, evals, data strategy, and how to become an AI PM.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The parts nobody teaches" icon="heart">
    Accountability without authority, decision traps, saying no, imposter syndrome, burnout, and first-person lessons from the trenches.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Curated from the best of GitHub" icon="github">
    Built on the strongest open PM resources, with every source and licence listed. Link-checked so the reading list does not rot.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open source, forever" icon="approve-check">
    Lessons are CC BY-SA 4.0, the site is MIT. Fix a typo, add a resource, or write a lesson with an "Edit page" click.
  </Card>
